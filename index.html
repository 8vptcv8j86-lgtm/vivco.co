One coordinated path

From a ready product
to a production plan.

Finding a manufacturer is only one part of scaling. Your formula, packaging, run size, quality requirements, budget and delivery date all need to work together.

VIVCO brings those pieces into one managed workflow. We assess your requirements, identify suitable licensed manufacturers and coordinate the work from preparation through delivery.

You retain ownership of your product and formula. The licensed manufacturer handles production and batch release. VIVCO manages the coordination, documentation and customer relationship.

For food & beverage brands

A practical route to your next production run.

Start with your product, its requirements and the market you want to serve. We build the production plan around them.

01

Prepare for production

Review formula documentation, product specifications, packaging, target volume and budget. Identify the formulation or technical support needed before requesting production quotes.

02

Match the right capacity

Assess manufacturer fit against process, package format, minimum run, certifications and timing. Coordinate supplier inputs and quotes so the production plan has clear costs and responsibilities.

03

Coordinate the run and delivery

Manage schedules, packaging and ingredient coordination, batch and quality records, freight arrangements and delivery confirmation, working alongside the licensed manufacturer.
